# Preserve native FOV in the curved source

`curve_native_eye` recovers a virtual render eye from the actual native perspective and the physical center chord panel. The projection already encodes Game/Realistic framing, dynamic native FOV and saved zoom offsets; no additional camera-set preference or FOV override is introduced. The real measured eye continues to define curve UVs. Feeding a fixed physical eye to source projection would cancel zoom changes, because inverse(originalP)*sourceP removes originalP from the final vertex position.

For center-panel width/height and near-plane bounds, horizontal/vertical eye distances must agree (aspect mismatch refuses). The eye is the panel midpoint minus its right/up times the normalized frustum-center offsets times distance, plus its eye-facing normal times distance. The center must use the native X/Y axes. In the centered case, eyeZ = virtual distance - measured eye-to-edge-plane distance. This does not subtract curve sagitta. The output stays unchanged on refusal.
