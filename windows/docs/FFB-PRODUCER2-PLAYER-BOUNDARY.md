# Boundary from producer@2 shadow to a selectable player model

Source audit at `1c2df54f4abc0c929f590c32927ffa9d8595859c`, following the one-run [ordinary software witness](https://github.com/d-b-c-e/dbce-project-mgmt/blob/main/artifacts/outrun-ffb-shadow-20261013/run-01/README.md). This is a design boundary, not a model selector, a settings migration, or permission to send force. The witness saw direct control, movement, signed steering and an F6 panel neutral/re-prime; it did not exercise a crash, tow, teleport, car replacement or physical wheel.

## Car continuity that exists now

`native_car_select.cpp` increments `race_inits` after successful `GamePlCar_Init` (`0x4A6ED0`) and clears `producer_active` on `GamePlCar` destruction (`0x49F4E0`). `race_player_car_control_4a8330` increments `producer_attempt` on every direct or nested call, then notifies the observer after the control body returns. The freshness gate requires a direct dispatch, active successful init, successful control, witnessed same-call `OperationInput` steering write, and the car record's own stale-steer gate. The shadow also resets on mode exit, pause depth, F6 open, failed freshness, a duplicate native tick pair, source-tick gap or new `race_inits`. The ordinary witness had one physical init/generation throughout, with consecutive fresh ticks on each side of F6; it cannot validate a mid-race car discontinuity.

The following **in-place** recovery paths can alter that same car work without a new `0x4A6ED0`, so `race_inits` alone cannot invalidate @2 history:

Before any physical @2 route, carry an explicit player-continuity invalidation from **every reached in-place relocation/recovery path** into the observer/model. Neutralize at the transition, discard model/shaper and gear history, and prime on the first proven direct control sample after recovery. A car-init counter, `+0xD50`, same-call steering write, or position-jump heuristic alone does not cover those paths. The current @2 CSV's `car_generation` means successful init count; do not silently reinterpret its versioned rows as a recovery counter. If a new token becomes part of the trace, version that schema. Source fixtures should exercise immediate tow, delayed countdown/snap, crash entry, duplicate/failed callbacks, pause and race exit with explicit zero/prime behavior. Then a muted ordinary runtime recovery observation should show the transition; no recovery observation exists yet. This is a finite call-site audit, not a reason to invent tyre signals.

## Settings and output boundary

The current path in `ffb_settings.cpp` parses the mod-derived `[FFB] FFBPreset` and `FFB*` keys into `LegacySettings`; `ffb_live.cpp` creates `LegacyForce` at the pre-update input filter, then sends its integer intent through `ffbdev::Delivery` only if `[FFB] Output = wheel` and all existing gates permit it. `desktop_panel.cpp` uses those same legacy keys for its FFB page. `Output` is a separate next-start choice, default `none`; `DBCE_FFB_MUTE`, process kind, wheel identity, front-window and Stop FFB/F8 gates remain authoritative.

@2's current `preview_model_settings()` instead fixes spring strength `0.30`, full-spring speed `30 m/s`, shift strength `0.05`, and all unavailable damping, lateral, tyre, texture and impact terms off. `preview_shaper_settings()` uses strength `50` (unity), inversion off, zero extra fade/smoothing/ramp. The observer computes after direct car control, a different seam from the legacy pre-update filter. No existing `[FFB]` key selects @2, no current @2 value reaches `ffbdev::Delivery`, and the owner's accepted legacy preset is not a valid automatic @2 tune.

A selectable implementation needs an **explicit, new, default-legacy model identifier** (for example `[FFB] Model = outrun-producer@2`, with the absent key retaining `outrun-legacy@1`); reject an unknown identifier without enabling output. Parse @2 settings into separate names/section and validate them independently, leaving all existing persisted keys, protocol names and legacy values untouched. Follow toolkit STD-027: expose one Steering strength (%) control at the shared reference of 50 for the steering/centering term, with an independent Shift strength (%) for the supported cue. Scale those terms before composition; keep the shaper gain fixed at unity (strength 50) instead of adding a redundant user-facing overall multiplier. The full-spring-speed reference and provisional 0.30 spring coefficient are internal model tuning, not another required Simple-page control. This adapter currently estimates centering from speed and steering, not measured tire force; say so. Keep inversion explicit and wheel polarity unqualified until an attended measurement. Keep unsupported legacy F6 controls hidden or clearly marked legacy-only when @2 is selected. Do not map legacy cornering load, grip loss, road texture, wall impact or damping into unavailable @2 signals.

Model selection must first remain software-only. A future post-control @2 delivery adapter would need to convert bounded shaped output to the existing device's integer range and deliver **zero on every invalidation/pause/stop**, without bypassing `ffbdev::Delivery` safety gates. The current shadow does not do this. Physical activation remains locked until the recovery boundary is witnessed, settings semantics and limits are fixed in fixtures, and the owner attends a muted-to-force A/B for wheel polarity, onset and stop behavior. The installed default, owner's tune and legacy trace remain as they are.
