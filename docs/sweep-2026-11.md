## For the owner to check

- **Widescreen race:** on the installed setup the race is 4:3 with black sides. Until ISS-0041 is fixed, the game's
  own Options > Settings aspect row (or `widescreen=1` in `options.ini`) should give a 16:9 race. Not tried in
  this sweep.
- **Driving with the wheel:** steer, throttle, brake and the H-pattern shifter in a race. The log shows 20 actions
  bound, but no wheel input was sent.
- **Wheel feel:** force feedback was muted for this run.
- **Wheel buttons in menus:** Confirm, Back and the navigation buttons on the wheel.
- **First start after install:** at the first start the old `[DirectInput]` keys are imported into `[Controls]`, and
  F6 then shows "No H-pattern input" until the next start (it showed "H-pattern, gears 1-6" in run 2). Restoring the
  ini means this happens again on your next start.
- **Triple screens:** with `[Triple] Screens = Separate monitors` (F6 > Cameras), the race spans all three screens and
  menus stay on the center. Judge whether the planar span looks right to you.
